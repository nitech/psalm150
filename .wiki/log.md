# Directory Update Log

## 2026-10-06
* **Song search is indexed**: migration `0010_song_search_trigrams.sql` turns on `pg_trgm` and adds GIN trigram indexes on revision title, authors, number and ChordPro, plus an index on `songs.current_revision_id`. `search_songs` now picks one of three statements (no search; with lyrics; without lyrics when `q` has no run of three letters or digits, which the index cannot look up), escapes `%`, `_` and `\` so they match themselves, and refuses `q` over 200 characters (400). With 30,000 public songs, a search took about 550 ms whatever it matched; now 0.2–11 ms with a three-letter word and under 100 ms for shorter ones. Pages touched: songs-and-revisions, data-model, index.
* **Security fixes from an audit**: the sign-in and password-change limits now count an attempt before its password is checked (`Throttle::attempt`, taken back with `forgive`/`clear` on success), so requests sent all at once no longer each get past the limit; at most four Argon2 hashes run at once (`HASH_SLOTS`), so a flood of sign-ins cannot exhaust memory; sign-in refuses usernames over 100 and passwords over 1024 characters before hashing, and new passwords are capped at 1024. An API token made through an expiring API token expires no later than it. An owner can no longer set their own password through `PATCH /members/{id}` (403; `PUT /account/password` needs the current one). Renaming a room now ends the streams of everyone following the old name instead of passing them the new one. `GET /remote/desktops/events` re-checks its token at most every 10 s rather than on every change. Every response carries `Content-Security-Policy`, `X-Content-Type-Options`, `X-Frame-Options` and `Referrer-Policy` (new tower-http `set-header` feature). Web client: every way a session ends clears the offline caches and the current set, and the service worker drops reads that were in flight at sign-out; `#/sets/new?song=` accepts only an id; a malformed `/join/%ZZ` link asks for the room instead of failing. Pages touched: auth-and-roles, rooms, web-client, deployment, index.
* **API reference in the web client**: `#/api` (also `/api/docs`, linked from a new More → About section with the OpenAPI document and the server version) reads `/api/openapi.json` and shows every operation by section with its description, whether it needs a token, parameters, request body, answers with their fields (nested types open in place) and a curl example with Copy; a search box filters and announces the count. The service worker keeps `/api/openapi.json` for offline reading. Every handler now has a doc comment, so every operation has a summary, and a new `Groups` modifier puts each operation in one section (`GROUPS` in `api.rs`, by path) and lists the sections as the document's tags, replacing the single "PSA150" tag. `openapi_describes_every_operation` now also fails on an operation without a summary or section. Pages touched: web-client, api-contract, index. `/AGENTS.md`, `/docs/api.md` and `/README.md` updated.

## 2026-10-05

New `--psa-on-danger` for text on `--psa-danger` (error toasts). `--psa-focus-ring` is now worked out on each element, so a part of a page that sets its own `--psa-focus-color` (the room's high contrast) gets a matching ring without redefining the ring. Mirrored to the desktop. Pages touched: accessibility.

Fields and segmented controls use `--psa-border-control`; "on" states get a 3:1 edge or bar, not only a tint; accent text on tints is mixed darker; list rows have an inset focus ring; `scroll-padding` keeps focus clear of the sticky bar, dock and step bar. Forms: password and room-name hints, errors tied to their field (`aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby`). `/join`: the reading tools are a labelled group, text size stays focusable at its ends and is announced, focus starts at the room name, high contrast has its own focus ring. New `web/src/a11y.ts` and `web/tests/a11y.test.ts`. New page [accessibility](concepts/accessibility.md).
* Pages touched: accessibility (new), web-client, build-and-ci, gaps, index.
* **Rooms**: every desktop has a room name, a random flower from `FLOWERS` (`rooms.rs`) given when its token is made (`room_name` in the `POST /device-tokens` and `POST /desktop-auth/token` answers) or, for older desktops, the next time they talk to the server. Anyone follows a room without a token at `GET /public/rooms/{room_name}` and its `/events` stream (`RoomView`: `live`/`blank`/`idle`/`offline`, words only while the desktop projects, shares (`shares_room`) and the church has rooms open; closed or unknown is 404). `/info` gains `rooms`, `GET`/`PUT /church` `public_rooms`. New page [rooms](concepts/rooms.md).
* **Remote live push**: `DesktopState` gains `shares_room` and `slides` (words without chords; 200 slides × 40 lines × 300 characters); `RemoteDesktop` gains `room_name` and is sorted by it. New `GET /remote/desktops/events` (any token) pushes the list as server-sent events on change, re-checked every 10 s, ending when the token stops working; the web Remote screens follow it with `fetch` instead of polling every 2 s and show room names, the current slide's words, rename and Copy/Share of the room link. Revoking a desktop drops it from the relay at once. Event streams send `X-Accel-Buffering: no`; proxies must not buffer them.
* **Web client**: `/join` and `/join/{room}` (served as `index.html` by the API) are a token-free room reader outside the shell: large text, whole song with the current slide followed, −/+, high contrast, follow toggle, screen-reader announcements, wake lock, last room remembered. Everyone gets **Change your password** (the lock beside their name). New `people` and `lock` icons in `shared/icons.ts`, mirrored to the desktop. The web client reads the session again with `GET /session` at boot and when it comes back into view (at most once a minute), so a changed role applies without signing in again.
* **Limits on wrong passwords** (`throttle.rs`, in memory): 5 per username and 30 per client address in 15 minutes at sign-in, 5 per person at password change; past a limit 429 with `Retry-After`, before any password is checked. New env var `PSA150_CLIENT_IP_HEADER` names the proxy's client-address header (last entry counts; IPv6 by /64); without it the connection's peer, now passed through `ConnectInfo`. Unknown usernames count too and are checked against a fixed Argon2 hash, so neither a 429 nor timing reveals usernames. `POST /setup` answers 409 before hashing once the server is set up.
* **Reusable usernames**: migration `0008_reusable_usernames.sql` lets `users.username` be null; removing someone from their last church keeps the disabled account but clears its username, so it can be given to someone new.
* **Desktop sign-out**: the Psalm150 desktop now revokes its own token with `DELETE /session` when it disconnects, opens a local folder or gets a new token (client repo, `server_library.rs`).
* Pages touched: auth-and-roles, api-contract, data-model, deployment, web-client, gaps, build-and-ci, overview, index. `/docs/api.md`, `/README.md`, `/TODO.md`, `/AGENTS.md`, `/.env.example` and `/compose.yaml` updated.
* **Everything through the API**: what was web-only or database-only is now in `/api/v1`, so anyone with a suitable token can do everything the server does. New `access.rs`.
* **API tokens**: `psa_a_` tokens a person makes for scripts and integrations (`GET`/`POST /api-tokens`, `DELETE /api-tokens/{id}`; migration `0007_api_tokens.sql`). A token acts as its person in one church with the lower of its own `role` and the person's current role, optionally until `expires_at`; it is never made with more than its maker has. Owners list and revoke everyone's.
* **Sessions and passwords**: `GET /session` says who a token is (`kind` `session`/`api`/`desktop`, role, expiry); `DELETE /session` revokes the token that sends it, whatever its kind. `PUT /account/password` (current password required) ends the person's other sessions. `POST /session` takes an optional `church_id`.
* **Members**: owners list, add, change (role, name, username, password, disabled) and remove people through `/members`, under the church row lock, keeping at least one owner who is not disabled; account fields of a person in another church are 403. Disabling and removal end sessions and revoke API tokens; an account left in no church is kept, disabled. `users.disabled_at` is now set.
* **Church, songs and sets**: `PUT /church` also renames (`name`), fields left out stay. `GET /songs/{id}/revisions/{revision_id}` reads any revision's source. `GET /songs/deleted` and `POST /songs/{id}/restore`, `GET /sets/deleted` and `POST /sets/{id}/restore` (a new version). `GET /sets` takes `offset`.
* **OpenAPI**: a `bearer` HTTP security scheme, marked on every operation that needs a token; `openapi_describes_every_operation` checks the eight public ones and that every documented operation is routed.
* **Web client**: sign-out (and "Use another account") sends `DELETE /session`; **More → API tokens** lists, makes (name and access up to your role) and revokes tokens. Fixed **Create a token by hand** naming the desktop token "null" (the form was read after it was disabled).
