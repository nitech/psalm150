Psalm150 exists to help song leaders and church members prepare, sing and share their songs, to the glory of the one Lord, Jesus Christ.

On Sunday

Everyone sings from the same page.

Words for everyone

White on black, as large as the screen allows and one size for the whole song. A line is never broken in the middle.

Chords for the musicians

A second screen shows the band the chords and what comes next, in the key you play it in.

On every phone

With a song server, anyone can follow the words on their own phone, as large as they need. No app and no account.

The desktop app

Made for the computer by the projector.

Search the song book, press Project, and the words are on the screen. Everything works on its own, with no server and no internet.

  • Your songs, offline

    A folder of ChordPro files is the song book. Keep it in Dropbox if you like; a whole service runs without the internet.

  • Sets for the service

    Put the songs in order during the week and step through them on Sunday.

  • A phone as the remote

    Change the slides from anywhere in the room.

  • Songs from the web

    When a song is missing, look it up, and lay the chords from the web over your own words.

  • Bring your old song book

    Psalm150 reads a Visual Song Book database, and can keep the two in step.

  • Updates, never mid-service

    New versions download in the background and install when you close the program.

The song server, if you want one

One song book for the whole church.

Put the church's songs on a small server and everyone shares them: every song with its history, sets for each service shared on a link, and an app for phones with chords you can tap to hear. Desktops keep their own copy, so a service never waits for the network.

  1. Find a computer. A Raspberry Pi is enough; anything that runs Docker will do.
  2. Start the server. 
    git clone https://github.com/nitech/psalm150.git
cd psalm150 && cp .env.example .env
docker compose up --build -d
    Choose a long password in .env first.
  3. Open it on port 8080 and set up your church.
How the song server works

Your songs are yours.

Every song is a plain ChordPro text file. Keep them, copy them, or take them to another program: nothing is locked in.
